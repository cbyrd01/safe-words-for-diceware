# Category gate (the positive model). A word is eligible only when its main
# noun sense falls under an `allow` category below. The most specific listed
# category wins, so a `deny` line carves a risky branch out of an allowed one
# (clothing inside covering, hoofed mammals inside animals).
#
# Format: allow|deny  wordnet-synset-id  # label: reason
#
# Risk tiers come from measurements on Open English WordNet: how often common
# words in the category carry a person/body/slur/sexual/drug sense, form
# unsafe compounds, or appear in profanity-list phrases. See
# docs/selection-model.md for the numbers. Proposed by AI (see README
# "AI use") and approved by the maintainer in October 2026; changes need
# human review.
